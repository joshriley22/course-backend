## Job and audience

Enrolled or prospective UVA student signing in or creating an account before using the planner.

## Outcome and proof

Primary action: submit the login or sign-up form. This brief is a direction note about the page's background treatment, not a form redesign.

## Selected direction

Background: a dimmed/blurred photograph of UVA's Lawn with the Rotunda centered behind the login/sign-up panel. This is a confirmed brand-recognition cue (grounds the app as UVA-specific) rather than a full visual-world decision — palette and type otherwise remain open.

## Scope and boundaries

`frontend/src/pages/Login.tsx` background only. Does not change the login/sign-up form's fields or logic (already known to be functionally incomplete — see PRODUCT.md).

## Interaction and layout

- Full-bleed background photo of the Lawn/Rotunda, dimmed and/or blurred so it reads as atmosphere rather than competing with the form.
- Login/sign-up panel stays centered over that background, matching the current layout.

## Constraints and open decisions

- The exact photo asset still needs sourcing — real UVA imagery, not a stock or generic substitute (per PRODUCT.md's policy against fabricated evidence/assets).
- Dim vs. blur strength, and whether both are applied together, is an open visual decision for implementation.
